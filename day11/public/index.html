Where bamboo meets coffee.

A new café on the first floor of Dream City Hotel, right by the ISBT flyover in Garchuk. Walnut slat walls, woven bamboo lamps, plants in every corner and big windows. Started by singer Anaya Brahma, so there's always a song somewhere.
