# Qasbah dish image prompts

Save each result into `public/img/dishes/` with the exact filename below (JPG, square, 2048x2048 or larger). The site picks them up automatically. Until a file exists, a plate illustration shows instead.

Use the same model and the same style block for all six so the set looks consistent. Good options: GPT Image 2, Seedream 5 Pro, Flux 3, Nano Banana.

## Shared style block (append to every prompt)

> Professional restaurant food photography, moody dark editorial look. Dark brass and charcoal table, shallow depth of field, warm golden light from the side like hanging Arabian lanterns, soft steam, tiny gold highlights, rich deep maroon and amber tones. The dish sits in the right half of the frame and the left third stays darker and simpler, because text is placed over it. 3/4 camera angle, 85mm lens, f/2. Photorealistic, appetizing, natural imperfections. No text, no logo, no watermark, no hands, no people.

## 1. `mutton-mandi.jpg`

Mutton Mandi: a large round brass platter of golden saffron-tinted long-grain basmati rice, topped with a whole piece of slow-cooked, fall-off-the-bone mutton shoulder, scattered with fried onions, toasted almonds, golden raisins and fresh coriander. Small bowl of red tomato chilli sauce beside it. Steam rising.

## 2. `chicken-al-faham.jpg`

Chicken Al Faham: whole charcoal-grilled Arabian chicken halves with deep red-orange spice marinade and charred edges, served on a dark slate board with a bowl of white garlic dip (toum), grilled tomato, grilled green chilli, lemon wedge and warm flatbread. Light smoke rising.

## 3. `kunafa.jpg`

Kunafa: a freshly baked round golden-orange kunafa with crisp shredded pastry crust, a slice cut out and lifted to show stretching melted white cheese inside, drizzled with glossy sugar syrup and topped with crushed pistachios. Small copper serving tray, rose petals on the side.

## 4. `malai-chicken-tikka.jpg`

Malai Chicken Tikka: creamy, pale ivory chicken tikka pieces on steel skewers with light char marks, glistening, served on a dark plate with sliced onion rings, green chutney in a small bowl, mint leaves and lemon. Faint smoke.

## 5. `dal-makhani.jpg`

Dal Makhani: a deep copper bowl of velvety dark black lentil curry, swirl of fresh cream and a knob of butter on top, garnished with a few coriander leaves and a slit green chilli. Butter naan torn beside it. Steam rising.

## 6. `mutton-rogan-josh.jpg`

Mutton Rogan Josh: a dark brass karahi of deep red, glossy Kashmiri-style mutton curry with tender bone-in mutton pieces, whole spices (cardamom, cinnamon, star anise) on the surface, a thin sheen of red oil, coriander garnish. Basmati rice and naan in the soft-focus background.
