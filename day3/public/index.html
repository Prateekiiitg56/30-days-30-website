About us

A fortress of flavour in Guwahati

In the Middle East and North Africa, a qasbah is a fortified citadel, a gathering place at the heart of the old town. Qasbah brings that spirit to Milan Nagar: a warm lounge where Middle Eastern cooking is served the way it is meant to be shared.

Slow-cooked Mutton Mandi, Arabian-spiced Chicken Al Faham and golden Kunafa, alongside tandoori favourites and North Indian classics, so there is something for every table.
