# 🍴 Flav Restaurant — menu, WhatsApp ordering and Swad, the AI host

> **Day 14** of the [30 Days, 30 Websites](https://github.com/Prateekiiitg56/30-days-30-website) challenge.

### 🔗 Live demo: **[flav-demo.vercel.app](https://flav-demo.vercel.app)**

A demo website for Flav Restaurant on Nayanpur Road, Guwahati. The look comes from the restaurant itself: the red and yellow of the logo, the deep green hedge wall, warm wood panels and the neon "My Flavour Hub" sign.

## ✨ Features

- 🔦 **Scroll walk** — five real photos of the restaurant (street sign, the neon wall, tandoor, momos and tikka, skewers) open one from the next like a light switching on.
- 🍽️ **Full menu with real prices** — every dish from the printed menu card, in tabs, with veg and non-veg markers and half / full or veg / egg / chicken options.
- 🛒 **Order cart** — add, remove and change quantities, see the total, then send the order to Flav's WhatsApp.
- 🌀 **3D bestsellers coverflow** — swipe or click through the dishes guests order first.
- 🪑 **Table request form** — date, time, guests and occasion go straight to WhatsApp.
- 🤖 **Swad, the AI host** — knows the menu and prices, answers in English, Hindi, Assamese or Bengali, and takes table and order requests.

Static HTML, CSS and JS on Vercel, plus one serverless function (`api/chat.js`). Set `OPENROUTER_API_KEYS` for the chat.
