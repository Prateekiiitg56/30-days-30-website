Good to know

Before you come.

What time is check-in?

Check-in and check-out are at 12 noon. Ask the team on WhatsApp if you need an early or late check-in.

What ID do I need?

A government photo ID for every guest aged 18 and above. Local IDs and unmarried couples are generally welcome.

Can we bring our pet?

Pets are not allowed on the property.

Is there parking?

Yes, a big free parking area right off NH-37.

Can we just come for lunch or the park?

Of course. The restaurant is open to everyone from 10 am to 10:30 pm, and families are welcome to use the park while they dine.

How do I get the best room rate?

Book direct: send your dates on WhatsApp and the team replies with the rate for your stay.
