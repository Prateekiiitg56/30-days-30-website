FAQ

Good to know.

Everything about visiting, booking, events and private occasions.

GENERAL

What is The Maroon Room?

A Pan-Asian fine dining restaurant and one of India's 100 best bars, with live music, bands, karaoke and signature cocktails.

Where are you?

5th floor of Aurus Mall, GS Road, Dispur, opposite Supermarket. Inox is on the same floor. There is ample free parking.

What are your opening hours?

12:30 pm to 12:30 am, every day. Hours may vary on event nights, so check the event listing before you come.

Is there an age restriction?

No, kids are always welcome. Friday to Sunday evenings can get loud with live music. Guests who wish to order alcohol may be asked for a valid photo ID; the legal age is 21 and above.

Is there a dress code?

Come dressed your best. Most guests dress to impress, but most importantly, come ready to relax and enjoy the night.

BOOKING

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome when space is available, but booking ahead is recommended, especially for live music, karaoke and weekend events. Reserve a table.

EVENTS

What kinds of events do you host?

Live bands, solo performers, themed music nights, karaoke, parties and special events. See the events page or Instagram for the latest.

When are karaoke nights?

Every Friday from 9 pm, sometimes earlier if the room is ready. Duets and group performances are welcome.

Can local bands and artists perform here?

Yes, the team loves discovering live talent. Send links to your music and social profiles and they will reach out.

PRIVATE EVENTS

Can I book The Maroon Room for a private event?

Yes, there are dedicated spaces for birthdays, celebrations, corporate gatherings, launches and live shows. The whole venue is not blocked for one party, so walk-in guests still enjoy their evening.

Can you arrange entertainment for my event?

Yes, depending on availability: live musicians, bands, DJs or karaoke. Tell us about your event.
